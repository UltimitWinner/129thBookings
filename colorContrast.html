Understanding Contrast Ratios & Legibility Scores

AAA Pass (Score 85–100)

Contrast ratio ≥ 7.0:1. Highest standard of readability. Fully accessible for small, medium, and large text regardless of user visual acuity.

AA Pass (Score 65–84)

Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1. Standard compliance requirement for normal body text under W3C WCAG 2.1 level AA guidelines.

AA Large / Fail (Score 0–64)

Contrast 3.0:1 to 4.49:1 passes only for large text (≥ 18pt or 14pt bold). Contrast below 3.0:1 fails WCAG standards for all body text.
